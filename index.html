About Milky Foods & Poultry

Established Commercial Poultry & Food Enterprise

Milky Foods and Poultry is a premier Pakistani agricultural and food production organization operating extensive poultry farming, processing, and wholesale supply operations. Built upon highest standards of biosecurity and animal care, we manage every phase from chick rearing to fresh meat delivery.

Our integrated business model guarantees consistent quality, strict Halal compliance, and superior hygiene. Serving commercial businesses, distributors, retail outlets, and restaurants across the country, we are proud to contribute to Pakistan's food security and agricultural excellence.
